## Product Roadmap

### September 2025

|Roadmap item|Remarks|
|---|---|
|KYC Modification|Ability to modify investor's data in the KRA database|
|Payment retry|Ability to retry payments against lumpsum purchase orders against lumpsum purchases|
|Calendar day Daily SIP|Daily SIP where installments are processed every day of the week irrespective of business holiday or not.|
|Switch orders|Ability to switch between different schemes of the same AMC. Distributors can switch from one Regular scheme to other and Advisors can switch from one direct scheme to other.|
|List fund schemes API|Access all schemes available via Cybrilla POA gateway.|

Note: All the capabilities will be available via FP-CybrillaPOA route.
